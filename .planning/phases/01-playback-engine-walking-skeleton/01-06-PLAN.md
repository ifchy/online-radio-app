# Phase 1 Plan 06: Station titles become clean Cyrillic now-playing values (windows-1251 repair, sanitising, junk filter)

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 1 Goal (verbatim): "A user on a physical Android phone can start a Bulgarian station and it keeps playing on its own through screen-off, background, calls, headphone changes, network switches and dead primary streams. The live edge, correct Cyrillic now-playing and media controls all work." (This line is not in user-story form; see 01-01-PLAN.md "Phase Goal".)

<objective>
Build the pure text pipeline that turns a raw ICY title into a `NowPlaying` value: windows-1251 repair, sanitising and clamping, the junk filter, and the "Artist - Title" split. Every step is proven by golden tests.

Purpose: STRM-05 and the text half of PLAY-02. Media3's IcyDecoder falls back to ISO-8859-1, so cp1251 titles arrive as mojibake (PITFALLS Pitfall 6; RESEARCH Pattern 5). These are pure functions with no engine dependency, so they are built and tested on their own in wave 2. Plan 01-08 wires them into the handler, the notification and the mini-player.
Output: the cp1251 table and repair, the sanitiser, `NowPlaying`, `parseIcyTitle`, and golden tests.
</objective>
