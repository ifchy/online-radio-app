# Phase 1 Plan 08: Correct Cyrillic "now playing" on the notification, lock screen and mini-player, never stale

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 1 Goal (verbatim): "A user on a physical Android phone can start a Bulgarian station and it keeps playing on its own through screen-off, background, calls, headphone changes, network switches and dead primary streams. The live edge, correct Cyrillic now-playing and media controls all work." (This line is not in user-story form; see 01-01-PLAN.md "Phase Goal".)

<objective>
Show ICY "now playing" artist/title on the mini-player, the notification and the lock screen, using 01-06's parser, with a guaranteed clear when the station changes, pauses, stops or fails.

Purpose: STRM-05 ("no stale title after switching") and the now-playing clause of PLAY-02. The native ICY state survives a load, so without a guard the previous station's title can show under the new one (PITFALLS Pitfall 6; RESEARCH Pattern 5, Pitfall E).
Output: `AudioEngine.nowPlaying`, the handler's ICY subscription and guards, the MediaItem subtitle, `nowPlayingProvider`, the mini-player line, and tests.
</objective>

| T-08-01 | Spoofing / Tampering | ICY title → MediaItem | medium | mitigate | Only parseIcyTitle output (sanitised, clamped, junk-filtered by 01-06) ever reaches the MediaItem; the handler never publishes raw ICY text. Unit-tested. |
| T-08-02 | Denial of Service | rapid metadata updates | medium | mitigate | Distinct-until-changed before republishing the MediaItem (Anti-Pattern 9); unit-tested (one publish for a duplicate title). |
| T-08-03 | Information integrity | stale title from the previous station | low | mitigate | Generation guard, pre-ready drop, clear on switch/pause/stop/error, and stop-before-load in the player; unit-tested. |
</threat_model>
