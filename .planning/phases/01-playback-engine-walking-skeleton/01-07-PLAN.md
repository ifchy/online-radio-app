# Phase 1 Plan 07: The radio behaves like a radio around calls, headphones and screen-off Wi-Fi, and Stop leaves nothing running

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 1 Goal (verbatim): "A user on a physical Android phone can start a Bulgarian station and it keeps playing on its own through screen-off, background, calls, headphone changes, network switches and dead primary streams. The live edge, correct Cyrillic now-playing and media controls all work." (This line is not in user-story form; see 01-01-PLAN.md "Phase Goal".)

<objective>
Route audio-focus and becoming-noisy events through the state machine: calls go to Interrupted and auto-resume (D-11), navigation prompts duck, another media app pauses, and unplugging pauses. Add the Wi-Fi lock platform channel, held only while actively playing or reconnecting. Handle a task swipe correctly. After this plan the whole FGS/lock/focus table in RESEARCH 319-328 holds.

Purpose: PLAY-05, PLAY-06, PLAY-10, PLAT-03 (60 min screen-off; just_audio holds no Wi-Fi lock) and PLAT-06 (no locks when not playing). It also carries the physical-device matrix for SC2 and SC4.
Output: the AudioSessionPort events and mapping, the Interrupted/Paused/duck transitions, the Wi-Fi lock (Kotlin + Dart), onTaskRemoved, and the state-invariant tests.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07-01 | Denial of Service (battery) | Wi-Fi lock held while not playing | high | mitigate | The lock is derived from state: held only in Connecting, Playing, Buffering and Reconnecting, and released in Interrupted, Paused, PlaybackError and Idle. Non-reference-counted. Covered by the invariant table test and the owner's post-Stop dumpsys check. |
| T-07-02 | Tampering (unwanted audio) | automatic resume after noisy / permanent focus loss | medium | mitigate | Only Interrupted + gainAfterPause may resume; noisy and permanent loss go to Paused with focus released; unit-tested (prohibition below). |
| T-07-03 | Denial of Service (FGS start refused on Android 12+/15+) | resume after a call | medium | mitigate | Interrupted keeps playing true, so the FGS is never dropped during a call (D-11, Pitfall 1). Resume-after-user-pause from Bluetooth or the lock screen is checked on the device (Pitfall G / A5). |
| T-07-04 | Elevation of Privilege | MethodChannel bg.izk.radio/wifi_lock | low | accept | The channel is in-process and reachable only by the app's own Dart code. It exposes only acquire, release and isHeld of the app's own lock. |
</threat_model>
