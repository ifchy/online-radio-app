# Phase 1 Plan 03: The app speaks Bulgarian or English, and an accessible mini-player controls playback

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 1 Goal (verbatim): "A user on a physical Android phone can start a Bulgarian station and it keeps playing on its own through screen-off, background, calls, headphone changes, network switches and dead primary streams. The live edge, correct Cyrillic now-playing and media controls all work." (This line is not in user-story form; see 01-01-PLAN.md "Phase Goal".)

<objective>
Add gen-l10n with Bulgarian and English ARB files (D-08) and the bottom mini-player (D-06) with its text state labels (D-09). The mini-player meets the TalkBack, 48 dp and large-text requirements.

Purpose: after the tracer (01-01) the only controls are the notification, the lock screen and the headset. This plan gives the listener in-app control and a readable state, in their language. It also creates every ARB key the phase needs, including the notification channel name that 01-07 uses.
Output: l10n.yaml, both ARB files and the committed generated localisations, `resolveAppLocale`, the localised station list, `MiniPlayer`, `stateLabelFor`, `FakeEngine`, and widget tests.
</objective>
