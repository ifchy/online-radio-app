# Phase 1 Plan 10: Drops and stalls come back at the live edge, with backoff and a bounded retry budget

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 1 Goal (verbatim): "A user on a physical Android phone can start a Bulgarian station and it keeps playing on its own through screen-off, background, calls, headphone changes, network switches and dead primary streams. The live edge, correct Cyrillic now-playing and media controls all work." (This line is not in user-story form; see 01-01-PLAN.md "Phase Goal".)

<objective>
Add the reconnect half of the state machine: the stall watchdog, reconnect with backoff and jitter at the live edge, and the `RetryBudget` presets with the online budget clock (D-10). The FGS stays alive while reconnecting, and every user command wins over a pending retry.

Purpose: PLAY-07 (drop and stall recovery, backoff, bounded budget, FGS alive), PLAY-10 and PLAY-11 during recovery, and PLAY-01 (the FGS stays while recovering and stops when the budget runs out). Network-change handling and the offline budget are wired in 01-12 on top of the clock built here.
Output: the retry budget presets, the reconnect policy and budget clock, the reconnect rows, the engine-level setting, and pure plus fakeAsync tests.
</objective>

    - Playing or Buffering + a player failure or a `completed` snapshot → Reconnecting, with InvalidateResolution and a backoff timer for the attempt. The retry starts at lastWorkingStreamIndex, then rotates.

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-10-01 | Denial of Service (battery and data drain) | reconnect loop | high | mitigate | RetryBudget presets (standard 3 min online), exponential backoff capped at 30 s with jitter, and full release (FGS, focus, timers) on exhaustion; unit-tested with fakeAsync. |
| T-10-02 | Denial of Service (OS kill mid-recovery) | FGS state during reconnect | high | mitigate | Reconnecting and a retry Connecting publish playing true, so audio_service keeps the FGS (Pitfall 1). The mapping and the handler are unit-tested. |
| T-10-03 | Tampering (unwanted playback) | pending timers after a user pause or stop | medium | mitigate | CancelAllTimers on pause and stop, the generation guard on every timer, and only UserPlay/UserResume may leave Paused, Idle or PlaybackError; unit-tested (PLAY-10). |
</threat_model>
